What is LibreOffice?

The full desktop office suite — Writer, Calc, Impress — reading and writing Word/Excel/PowerPoint formats. It covers part of what Microsoft 365 does — review the known gaps below before committing.

What it covers

  • ✓ Documents, sheets, slides
  • ✓ DOCX/XLSX/PPTX support
  • ✓ Offline-first

Known gaps

  • ▲ Complex macro-heavy files may reflow
  • ▲ No equivalent cloud co-editing out of the box

Switching from Microsoft 365

Keep files in open formats (.odt/.ods) when possible; DOCX round-trips well for typical documents.

Tip: run both tools side by side on one real project for two weeks before cutting over — switching cold is the most common regret.
