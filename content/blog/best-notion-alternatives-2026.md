Notion's Plus plan runs about **$96/year per person** (billed annually). If you only use it for docs, kanban boards and a personal wiki, open-source replacements cover that ground for $0 — and keep your data in formats you control.

## The headliner: AFFiNE

[AFFiNE](/affine) is our top-rated Notion alternative because its core loop matches what most people actually use Notion for:

- Rich documents with slash commands
- Databases and kanban views
- An infinite whiteboard canvas
- Realtime collaboration

It blends all three surfaces into one workspace, which is exactly Notion's pitch — minus the vendor lock-in.

### What it does not cover (yet)

Honesty first: AFFiNE's parity list on this site flags gaps around Notion's more exotic features — think advanced formula properties and the API/plugin ecosystem. Check the [AFFiNE profile](/affine) for the current community-reported list before migrating a critical workspace.

## Also worth a look

- **[Focalboard](/focalboard)** — if your Notion usage is really "Trello with extra steps", Focalboard (from the Mattermost team) is a focused kanban/project board.
- **[NocoDB](/nocodb)** — when your Notion databases grew into actual business tooling, NocoDB turns any database into a spreadsheet-style Airtable/Notion-hybrid interface.

## How to switch safely

1. Export your Notion workspace to Markdown & CSV (**Settings → Export**).
2. Import into AFFiNE — Markdown imports are supported natively.
3. Run both tools side-by-side for two weeks before cancelling (the "trial sprint" rule).
