# Landing Pages Skill

> Build high-converting landing pages with proven layouts — hero sections, CTAs, social proof, and mobile-first design

## Install

```bash
npx skills add YepAPI/skills --skill landing-pages
```

Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Copilot, and [45+ more agents](https://github.com/vercel-labs/skills).

## What This Skill Does

Teaches your AI agent to generate landing pages that actually convert. It covers the proven section rhythm (hero, problem, solution, features, social proof, pricing, final CTA), benefit-driven headlines, single primary CTA per section, mobile-first responsive layouts, and performance optimization for above-the-fold content. Your agent will also apply SEO best practices with `generateMetadata()` for every landing page it builds.

## Key Features

- **Hero Section Patterns** — Benefit-driven headline, supporting subheadline, primary CTA button, and hero image/illustration positioned above the fold where they drive action
- **Conversion-Optimized Layout** — Proven section flow from hero through problem, solution, features, social proof, pricing, and final CTA — each section building toward the conversion
- **Social Proof Placement** — Testimonials, logo bars, user counts, and ratings strategically placed near CTAs where they reduce friction and build trust
- **Mobile-First Responsive** — Text before images, stacked sections, thumb-friendly CTA buttons, and minimal navigation to reduce exit points on small screens
- **Performance Optimized** — WebP hero images with proper sizing, lazy-loaded below-fold content, and sticky CTA on scroll for fast load times and high engagement

## Use Cases

- Launching a new product with a waitlist page that captures emails and builds hype
- Building a startup landing page that explains what you do and drives signups
- Creating campaign-specific pages for paid ads with focused CTAs and no distractions
- Shipping a pricing page with feature comparisons, testimonials, and clear upgrade paths
